About Jinovice

We started Jinovice with a simple idea: shopping online should feel as trustworthy as buying from a store you know. That means genuine products, honest information and service that doesn’t disappear after checkout.

Our first focus is beauty and skincare, a category where quality and authenticity matter most. As we grow, we’ll bring the same care to new product ranges for everyday living.

What we do

We handle the full journey of a product, from finding it to putting it in a customer’s hands.

Product sourcing

We work directly with brands and authorised distributors to find products worth recommending.

Online retail

We sell through our own channels and leading online marketplaces, with clear and accurate product information.

Fulfilment and care

We pack, ship and support every order, and we stay available when customers need help.

What we stand for

  • Genuine products

    Everything we sell comes from the brand or an authorised supplier. No exceptions.

  • Honest information

    We describe products clearly and accurately, so customers can choose with confidence.

  • Lasting relationships

    We aim to earn repeat customers and long-term brand partners, not one-off sales.
